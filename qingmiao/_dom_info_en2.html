About Qingmiao Intelligent

Ningbo Qingmiao Intelligent Technology Co., Ltd. ('Qingmiao Intelligent') was founded on February 2, 2021. It is a key incubated tech company of 'One Academy, One Center' and a startup incubated by Zhejiang University. Its headquarters are in Yinzhou District, Ningbo, Zhejiang, located in the eZhou Smart Industrial Park.

The company focuses on auto parts and cross-border e-commerce, positioning itself as a 'Four-in-One supply chain (informatization, automation, digitalization, intelligence) solution provider'. Its core products include OMS/WMS/TMS, with WMS (warehouse management system) as a core strength.

Business Scope

Technical services, technology development, consulting, exchange, transfer, and promotion; sales of intelligent material handling equipment; information system integration; supply chain management services; AI application software development; software development; software outsourcing; intelligent robot sales; industrial robot sales; intelligent robot R&D; import and export of goods.

Implementation Approach

Unlike traditional system delivery, Qingmiao Intelligent builds a 'targeted design, companion delivery, metric-based implementation' model. The company deeply integrates business and technology by connecting MES and WMS and using a multi-protocol WCS control system for end-to-end coordination of production execution and logistics equipment.

Team Strength

The company has dozens of senior consultants and technical experts with more than 10 years of experience in manufacturing, software, retail distribution, express logistics, and supply chain. It is one of China's most professional logistics informatization service teams in the circulation field.
