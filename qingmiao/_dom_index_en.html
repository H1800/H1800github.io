About Qingmiao Intelligent

Ningbo Qingmiao Intelligent Technology Co., Ltd. was founded in 2021. It is a key incubated tech company of 'One Academy, One Center' and a startup incubated by Zhejiang University. Its headquarters are in Yinzhou District, Ningbo, Zhejiang, located in the eZhou Smart Industrial Park.

The company focuses on auto parts and cross-border e-commerce, positioning itself as a 'Four-in-One supply chain (informatization, automation, digitalization, intelligence) solution provider'. Its core products include OMS/WMS/TMS supply chain management systems.
